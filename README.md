# 🚀 Career Compass AI

Career Compass AI is an AI-powered career guidance platform designed to help students explore careers, discover job opportunities, build learning roadmaps, and make better career decisions.

## 🎯 Objective

The main objective of Career Compass AI is to provide students with a single platform for:

- 🧭 Career exploration
- 🎯 Career recommendations
- 💼 Job exploration
- 📚 Learning roadmaps
- 🤖 AI-based career guidance
- 🚀 Project exploration
- 📊 Career comparison

## ✨ Features

### 🧭 Career Explorer
Explore different career paths, required skills, qualifications, and career information.

### 🎯 Career Recommendation
Get career recommendations based on student interests, skills, and preferences.

### 💼 Job Explorer
Explore available job roles and related career opportunities.

### 📚 Learning Roadmap
Follow structured learning paths to develop the skills required for different careers.

### 🤖 AI Mentor
Get AI-based guidance and suggestions for career-related questions.

### 🚀 Project Explorer
Discover project ideas that help students improve their practical skills.

### 📊 Compare Careers
Compare different career options and understand their requirements.
